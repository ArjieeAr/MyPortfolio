Every developer needs a home on the web, so my first project is this site. Here's how I approached it.

## Why Astro

Astro builds pages ahead of time and sends plain HTML to the browser, with JavaScript only where it's actually needed. For a portfolio that's mostly text and links, that means a fast site with very little to go wrong. It also lets me write blog posts like this one as simple Markdown files.

## Why Tailwind CSS

Tailwind let me style everything directly in my components with small utility classes. I didn't have to switch back and forth between files, and it was easy to keep spacing and colors consistent.

## What I focused on

- **Easy to update.** My projects live in a single data file, so adding a new one doesn't mean touching the layout.
- **Accessibility.** Semantic HTML, keyboard-friendly navigation, visible focus styles, and readable color contrast in both themes.
- **Dark and light mode.** Dark by default, with a toggle that remembers your choice.
- **Responsive design.** The layout works on phones first, then scales up.

## What's next

Now that the foundation is in place, I'll keep adding projects as I build them and writing about what I learn along the way.
